Little Linden (provisional brand) · Phase 3 · Hero lab

Ten hero concepts, awaiting your pick

Each card opens an isolated, working demo built with Three.js (WebGL / GLSL) and GSAP. Only one WebGL scene is ever loaded at a time. Each demo has cursor tilt, a spotlight and foil product card, an animated conic CTA border, parallax depth, blur-to-clear reveals, and animated icons with a neutral counter. Inside a demo, use the bar at the bottom to switch between concepts.

  • Prototype only — not integrated into a Shopify theme yet
  • All copy, products and props are DEMO placeholders
  • Thumbnails captured in headless Chromium (software GPU)
